# Clipes de IA para a cena "Ela é inteligente"

Gere os 2 clipes na sua ferramenta de vídeo com IA (Veo, Kling, Runway, Sora etc.), **vertical 9:16**, 5 a 8 s cada, sem texto, sem legenda e sem áudio. Salve como:

- `video-ads/clips/a.mp4`
- `video-ads/clips/b.mp4`

Depois rode `python assemble.py nexusfi-atelie-da-ni-v11.mp4` dentro de `video-ads`. A cena usa o clipe A nos primeiros 2,9 s e o clipe B no resto, com o texto por cima.

## Clipe A: lançando produtos no marketplace

> Vertical 9:16, cinematic, shallow depth of field. A confident Brazilian woman in her 30s, brown wavy hair, wearing a casual blazer, sits at a bright craft studio desk full of handmade stationery, notebooks and colorful paper products. She photographs a product with her smartphone, then types on a laptop and publishes the listing on an online marketplace. A small satisfied smile. Warm natural window light, handheld camera with a slow push-in. No text on screen.

## Clipe B: finanças na mão (o problema)

> Vertical 9:16, cinematic, shallow depth of field. The same woman at the same desk, now overwhelmed: stacks of paper receipts, a handwritten ledger notebook, a pocket calculator and a pen. She writes numbers by hand, recounts them on the calculator, rubs her forehead, sighs and crosses out a line. Slightly cooler, moodier light, handheld camera, slow push-in on her face. No text on screen.

Dica: peça a mesma personagem nos dois clipes (use o primeiro quadro do clipe A como imagem de referência para o B).
